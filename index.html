my story

i like figuring out how systems are wired, where the data goes sideways and why "it works on my machine" is almost never the full story. most of the time that means node.js, python, a database that's having a bad day, and the occasional map. i've built dashboards for urban transportation, backends for real business problems nobody warned me about, and geospatial tools for environmental data — and somewhere along the way i also wired up a raspberry pi, because apparently breaking things in software wasn't enough.

over time i've come to suspect that "working code" and "well-thought-out systems" are two very different animals, and i'm slowly learning to tell them apart.

this site is where i keep the things i've built, broken, rebuilt, and occasionally gotten right on the first try (rare, but it happens). stick around — there's probably something here worth poking at.
