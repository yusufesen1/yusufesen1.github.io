my story

i like figuring out how systems work. over time i've realized that how something works matters as much as what it produces. most of the time that means node.js, python, a database and the occasional map. i've built dashboards for urban transportation, backends for real business problems, map-based tools for environmental data and data-driven applications that support decision-making. lately i've been focused on figuring out how to meaningfully fold ai into these systems. somewhere along the way i also got into raspberry pi because apparently writing code wasn't enough.

over time i've become convinced that "working code" and "well-built systems" are two entirely different animals, and i'm still learning to tell them apart.

this site is where i keep the things i've built, broken, rebuilt, and occasionally gotten right on the first try (rare, but it happens). stick around — there's probably something here worth poking at.
